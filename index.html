Define the problem first

Three questions for a better project brief.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Where does work get stuck?

Identify the exact task, the people involved and the information they need. Describe the delay or repeated effort in concrete terms.

02 / CONNECT

Which systems are involved?

List the applications, data owners and existing connections. Clarify which records are authoritative when information differs across platforms.

03 / EVALUATE

What would success look like?

Agree on measurable acceptance criteria. Establish a starting point so the team can assess whether a change improves the intended process.

From discussion to a clear scope

A practical engagement checklist.

Planning Area Questions to Resolve Useful Deliverable
Current process What happens today, and where are the manual steps? A documented workflow and baseline.
Data readiness Are records complete, consistent and accessible? A data inventory with known gaps.
Responsibilities Who owns decisions, implementation and ongoing maintenance? A written responsibility matrix.
Automation boundaries Which actions need approval, and what happens when a task fails? Permission rules and an exception process.
Validation How will the proposed workflow be assessed before wider use? A bounded pilot with acceptance criteria.
